# Trace a cropped exhibition photo

Synthetic example. A cropped repost shows a locomotive nameplate behind glass. The task asks for the earliest confirmed publication of that frame.

1. OCR of the nameplate returns two possible readings. A text search using the museum name visible on a second sign resolves the spelling.
2. Reverse search of the nameplate crop finds an older photo of the same locomotive. In that photo the glass reflects a ceiling lamp in a different position and the frame lacks a chipped paint mark visible in the supplied crop.
3. A wider crop retaining the window edge leads to a museum article and a later magazine story. Both show the same lamp reflection, paint chip, and diagonal edge. The magazine credits the museum and links to its article.
4. The museum article displays 2 June. The earliest available snapshot showing the exact frame is 4 June. The magazine snapshot is from 19 June. Report the museum article as the earliest confirmed publication found, with its stated date and the first capture that establishes the image was present. The time of photography remains unknown.
