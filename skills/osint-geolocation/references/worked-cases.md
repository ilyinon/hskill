# Choose between two riverside viewpoints

Synthetic example. A photo shows a footbridge at the center of the frame, a square tower to its right, and a short stairway immediately in front of the camera. A legible sign gives the town name but no coordinates.

1. Both town promenades have stairs and views toward a bridge. The tower is visible from each. Similar stonework initially makes the north promenade look plausible.
2. Looking toward the bridge from the north stairs puts the tower to the left. From the south stairs it is to the right, and a map shows a service path aligned with the bridge's near pier as in the photo.
3. A dated south-bank panorama confirms the shape of the stairs. A second plausible spot farther along the same bank loses the alignment between the path and pier.
4. Report the south stair landing and the direction toward the bridge. The photo can be located to the landing. Its exact position within the landing and its recording date remain uncertain.
